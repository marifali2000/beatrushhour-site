Common questions

How does BeatRushHour pick the best time?

You choose the earliest time you could leave, and BeatRushHour checks the predicted drive time across the hour after it (live traffic from TomTom), then picks the quickest departure. Set the alarm and it keeps checking until it's time, moving the alarm if a better time appears.

The alarm didn't make a sound

On iOS 26 or later, turn on Settings → Alarm sound → Ring until stopped, even on silent and allow alarms when asked; the alarm then rings like the Clock app's, even on silent or in a Focus. If you said no before, turn alarms on in your iPhone's Settings → Apps → BeatRushHour. On older iOS versions the phone plays the sound once, and not when it's on silent.

Where do road alerts, weather and events come from?

Road closures, crashes and roadwork come from Ontario 511 (511on.ca) and the City of Toronto, weather from Environment and Climate Change Canada, and big games and concerts from Ticketmaster. Each alert links to its official page. Times are as published by the source.

Why do I need an authenticator app to sign in?

Two-step sign-in keeps your saved places and trips private even if your password leaks. Any authenticator app works (for example Apple Passwords, Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator).

I've used today's traffic lookups

Each account has a daily allowance of traffic lookups, which resets overnight (UTC). The gauge at the top of the Plan screen shows what's left; a longer check interval in Settings uses fewer.

Where does it work?

Trip planning works anywhere in Canada. Road alerts, weather and events cover Ontario and the Greater Toronto Area.

Delete your account

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your sign-in and everything linked to it at once. If you can't sign in any more, email us from your account's address and we'll delete it for you. See the Privacy Policy for details.
