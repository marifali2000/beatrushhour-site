Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

BeatRushHour tells you the best time to leave for a drive in the Greater Toronto Area. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who it's shared with, and how to delete it.

In short: no ads, no tracking across apps or websites, and we never sell your data. We keep your account, your saved places and alarms, and (if you leave sharing on) traffic readings without addresses and with locations rounded to about 100 m, and which features you use, to improve predictions. You can turn sharing off, and you can delete your account and everything linked to it from inside the app at any time.

We don't collect your contacts, photos, health data, or your location in the background, and we don't use advertising identifiers.

Who we share it with

We use these service providers to run the app. They get only what they need for their part, and none of them may use it for advertising:

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and traffic server (data stored in the United States).
  • TomTom receives the start and end points of trips you plan and the text you type into address search, to return routes, drive times and addresses.
  • OpenFreeMap serves the map tiles your phone downloads (like any website, it sees your internet address and which area you're viewing).
  • Ontario 511 (511on.ca) serves the live traffic camera images your phone loads, and the official alert pages you open.
  • Waze, Google Maps or Apple Maps receive your destination when you tap Start drive.

Our server also reads public data that involves none of your information: road alerts from Ontario 511 and the City of Toronto, weather from Environment and Climate Change Canada, and events from Ticketmaster (we ask for a day's events in the GTA; nothing about you is sent). Links to an event's Ticketmaster page open in your browser.

We may disclose information if the law requires it. We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Your choices

  • Sharing: turn off Settings → Account → Share anonymous traffic and usage data. Traffic readings and usage events stop, and anything still waiting to upload is discarded.
  • Location: allow or deny it in your phone's Settings at any time. The app works without it.
  • Notifications and alarms: turn them off in your phone's Settings.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete account removes your sign-in and, at once, everything linked to it: saved places, weekly alarms, traffic readings, usage events, answers and lookup counts.
  • Access or correction: email us and we'll send you a copy of your data or correct it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete your account, except the server's short-lived cache, which expires within a day. Copies in our hosting provider's backups are removed when those backups expire.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Two-step sign-in protects every account, and the database lets each person read only their own rows. No method is perfect, but we work to protect your information and will tell you promptly if a breach affects you.

Children

BeatRushHour is meant for drivers and isn't directed at children under 16. We don't knowingly collect their information; if you believe a child has given us data, email us and we'll delete it.

Where your data is

We're based in Ontario, Canada, and your data is stored in the United States by our hosting provider, where it may be accessible to authorities under US law. We follow Canada's privacy law (PIPEDA).

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app before significant changes take effect.
